Last updated: 9 September 2026 · See also our Cookie Policy

Who is responsible

The data controller is Danny Nissani, operating under GoBizIT (Spain). Contact: agents@gobizit.ai.

What we collect and why

Join / registration form: if you register on the Join page we collect your first name, email address and how often you want to hear from us (daily, weekly, monthly). We use it to send you the journal by email at the frequency you chose and to give you access to the journal pages. Legal basis: your consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)), which you can withdraw at any time via the unsubscribe link in every email or by writing to us. A copy of your registration is also kept in your own browser's local storage so the site remembers you (see the Cookie Policy).

Live chat: the chat bubble is provided by Zoho SalesIQ. If you accept cookies in the chat banner, Zoho records your visit (pages viewed, approximate location from your IP address, browser) and any messages and contact details you type into the chat, so we can answer you and follow up. Legal basis: consent for the cookies, and our legitimate interest in replying to you (Art. 6(1)(f)) for the conversation itself. Details of the cookies are in the Cookie Policy.

Email you send us: we receive whatever you write to us and use it only to reply.

Server logs: our hosting provider (Vercel) keeps standard, short-term server logs (IP address, browser type, timestamp) for security and abuse prevention. They are not used to identify you.

We do not run advertising trackers, sell data, or profile you for marketing beyond the email frequency you asked for.

Who we share it with

We use a small number of processors under GDPR-compliant contracts: Zoho (SalesIQ chat and Zoho Campaigns for sending the journal emails; Zoho CRM for the subscriber list), Vercel (hosting) and Google (web fonts on some pages). Some of these providers process data outside the EU/EEA; those transfers are covered by the EU Standard Contractual Clauses in each provider's data-processing terms. We do not share your data with anyone else unless the law requires it.

How long we keep it

Registration and email subscription: until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it, then removed from the active list within 30 days.

Chat transcripts: kept in Zoho SalesIQ for up to 12 months, then deleted.

Emails you send us: kept while the conversation is relevant, then deleted.

Server logs: kept short-term by the hosting provider for security only.

Your rights (EU/EEA users)

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of your personal data, to data portability, to object to processing, and to withdraw consent at any time. To exercise any of these rights — or if you have a question about this policy — email agents@gobizit.ai.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority; in Spain this is the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos.

Cookies

Which cookies we use, what they do and how to refuse them is explained in our separate Cookie Policy.
