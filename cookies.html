1. What this policy covers

This policy explains what cookies and similar technologies (such as browser local storage) the website makemoney.gobizit.ai uses, why we use them, how long they last, and how you can accept, refuse or withdraw your consent. It is written to comply with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the ePrivacy Directive and, because we operate from Spain, Article 22.2 of the Spanish Information Society Services Act (LSSI-CE) and the cookie guidance of the Spanish Data Protection Authority (AEPD).

2. What cookies are

A cookie is a small text file that a website stores on your device when you visit it. Browser local storage works in a similar way but does not get sent to a server automatically. Cookies can be first-party (set by this site) or third-party (set by another domain); session (deleted when you close the browser) or persistent (kept until they expire or you delete them).

5. Legal basis

Strictly necessary storage is used on the basis of our legitimate interest in providing the service you asked for (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f); LSSI-CE Art. 22.2 exemption). All other cookies are set only with your consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a); LSSI-CE Art. 22.2), which you give or refuse in the chat consent banner. Refusing is as easy as accepting, and refusing never blocks access to the site.

6. How to manage or withdraw consent

  • In your browser: you can block or delete cookies and site data at any time. Instructions: Chrome · Firefox · Safari · Edge. Blocking all cookies may stop the chat from remembering your conversation.
